    Hello Mayhemers! In this patch, we're focusing on bugfixes and small quality-of-life improvements.     Augments

      NEW   Archmage

    • Whenever you cast an ability, refund the cooldown of another, randomly selected ability by 30% of the cast ability's cooldown.

      NEW   Crit N Cast

    • Gain Ability Haste equal to 40% of your Critical Strike Chance.

      NEW   Death Dealer

    • Replacement: Replaces Flee.
    • Activate on a target champion to enter the Death Realm for 4 / 8 seconds. Entering the Death Realm causes you to become invisible for the first 2 seconds.
    • Cap Stone: At Max Level, killing your target grants your team another revive and delays the slain player's revive by 1 second.

      NEW   Got That Dog In Em

    • Every 3 seconds spawn a nearby hot dog. This can be picked up by anyone, causing them to breathe fire.
    • Enemies who pick this up can damage their allies.
    • If you die while breathing fire, you explode, dealing magic damage per stack.

      NEW   Hellbent

    Generate a stack of Revivemaxxing for each enemy champion you hit with basic attacks or abilities, lasting for 6 seconds, refreshing on subsequent hits, and stacking up to 10 times. At maximum stacks, you become Hellbent.

    • Hellbent: Upon taking lethal damage, you enter resurrection for 4 seconds, during which you are invulnerable, untargetable, and unable to act. Afterwards, you revive with 35 - 75% (based on level) of your maximum health with a 90-second cooldown. Additionally, you become empowered for the next 15 seconds, granting you 15 - 120 (based on level) bonus Attack Damage or 25 - 200 (based on level) Ability Power (Adaptive), 20% - 40% (based on level) bonus Move Speed while facing toward nearby enemy champions, and 15% - 30% (based on level) Omnivamp for the duration.

      NEW   Nature Is Healing

    Upon entering a patch of brush, you are healed for 40 - 300 (based on level) (+15% of your missing health) over 1.5 seconds. This has a 10-second cooldown per brush patch.

      NEW   Rite of Ascension

    Scoring a takedown against an enemy champion within 3 seconds of damaging them causes them to leave behind their Essence for 8 seconds. You can basic attack the Essence within 400 units to consume it, causing you to blink to its location after winding up for 1.25 seconds, during which you are untargetable, displacement immune, and unable to act.

    • Upon consumption of the Essence, you gain 100% bonus Move Speed decaying over 2 seconds, heal yourself for 75 - 300 (based on level) (+50% bonus AD) (+25% AP), and reset the cooldowns of your basic abilities. The heal is increased by 0% - 100% (based on your missing health), for a maximum heal of 150 - 600 (based on level) (+100% bonus AD) (+50% AP).

      NEW   Shark Bait

    Upon taking fatal damage, you become a Shark Bait, causing you to enter a zombie state for 3.5 seconds, during which your Move Speed is set to a static 400, you gain Ghosting, and enemies within 400 units of you are slowed by 40%.

    • At the end of the duration, a massive shark emerges from beneath you that lets out a powerful chomp in a 400 radius, dealing 120 - 350 (based on level) (+10 / 30% of the target's missing health) magic damage to all enemies within the area and knocking them up for 1 second.
    • While under the zombie state, you are able to move and become invulnerable, untargetable, and immune to crowd control, but you are also rendered unable to declare basic attacks, cast abilities, use summoner spells, and activate items.
    • At Max Level, everyone on your team will turn into a shark on death.

      NEW   Sonata

    Alternate between automatically casting a modified version of Sona's Aria of Perseverance and modified Song of Celerity, with the former being cast first on a 7.5-second cooldown.

    • Aria of Perseverance: Heal yourself for 60 (+60% AP) and send out a tone to the most wounded allied champion within 1000 units that heals them for the same amount. Additionally, generate an aura that grants you and tagged allied champions an 80 (+40% AP) shield for 1.5 seconds.
    • Song of Celerity: Gain 30% bonus Move Speed for 7 seconds. If you take damage during this time, the duration ends prematurely once or if 3 seconds have elapsed. Additionally, generate an aura that grants tagged allied champions 20% bonus Move Speed for 3 seconds.

      NEW   Surge Field

    Upon casting your ultimate ability, you spawn a surge zone beneath you that has a 300 radius. While you are within the zone, you gain 50 Ability Haste and 40% bonus Move Speed, and your damage dealt to enemy champions fires missiles at them that deal 20% of the triggering damage instance pre-mitigation as bonus magic damage.

    • The missile effect cannot trigger from itself.
    • The zone lasts indefinitely as long as you stay within its radius.
    • If you leave the zone's radius for the first time, it moves to your current location.
    • Exiting the zone a second time causes it to disappear if you do not re-enter the area within 1.25 seconds.

      NEW   Titan's Resolve

    Dealing and receiving champion damage grants you 1 stack of Juggernauting for 6 seconds. For every 10 stacks of Juggernauting you gain 25-75 Adaptive Force, 5-10 Armor and Magic Resist, Size, and 8% Tenacity.

    • Rank 1 Stacks Gained: 1
    • Rank 2 Stacks Gained: 2

      NEW   Warlock Juicebox

    Gain Omnivamp based on your Ability Power.

      NEW   Wee Woo Wee Woo

    Gain up to 50% Move Speed toward nearby allies based on their missing Health. Your healing and shielding are also increased up to 50% based on the target's missing Health.
